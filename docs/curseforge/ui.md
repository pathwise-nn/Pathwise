# Pathwise UI

**Your whole interface, in your color.**

Unit frames, action bars, tooltips, fonts and the game's
windows for World of Warcraft: Forever, in one clean look. Works on its own;
with [Pathwise](https://github.com/pathwise-nn/pathwise) installed too, the
two share one accent color.

**Beta.** This is Pathwise UI's first release and it has not yet been played
end to end in Forever. Tell us on Discord what looks or works wrong.

## Healing frames: Pathwise Heal

Click-to-heal party and raid frames, like HealBot or VuhDo, are their own
download: **Pathwise Heal**. It works with or without Pathwise UI; with both
installed they share one accent.

## Unit frames

You, your target, its target, your pet, your focus and your party: health in
your class color, power, names, levels, short numbers ("12.3k"), debuffs,
cast bars, combat and leader marks, portraits. Click to target, right-click
for the menu, in combat too. Move and size them.

## Action bars

Five bars of the game's own secure buttons, so your spells and keys stay
where they are. The main bar follows your stance, form or stealth. Buttons
per row, size, spacing, fade out of combat. Plus a thin experience bar, the
game's menu and bag buttons in two neat rows, and your money, always shown.

## Your look

- Your color: Pathwise blue, your class color, or any color you pick. Six
  color schemes, every color your own, and codes to share a scheme.
- Tooltips with a flat ground and a border in the item's quality or the
  player's class color; Inter for all the game's text; the game's windows
  on a flat ground with your accent along the top.
- Smooth motion, crisp one-pixel lines at any UI scale, and one button to
  move everything at once.
- Settings for every character, or each its own. Options: `/pwui` or the
  minimap button.
