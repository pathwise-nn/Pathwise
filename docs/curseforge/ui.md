# Pathwise UI

**Your whole interface, in your color.**

Unit frames, action bars, nameplates, tooltips, fonts and the game's windows
for World of Warcraft: Forever, in one clean look. Works on its own; with
[Pathwise](https://github.com/pathwise-nn/pathwise) installed too, the two
share one accent color.

**Beta.** This is Pathwise UI's first release on its own page and it has not
yet been played end to end in Forever. Tell us on Discord what looks or works
wrong.

## Healing frames: Pathwise Heal

Click-to-heal party and raid frames, like HealBot or VuhDo, are their own
download: **Pathwise Heal**. It works with or without Pathwise UI; with both
installed they share one accent.

## Unit frames

- **You, your target, its target, your pet, your focus and your party**:
  health in your class color, power, names, levels, short numbers ("12.3k"),
  debuffs, cast bars, combat and leader marks, portraits. Click to target,
  right-click for the menu, in combat too. Move and size them.
- **Two looks**: Slim (the name and health on a dark ground over slim bars)
  or Full bars (the bars fill the frame, the text on them).
- **Your cast bar** over your action bars, in place of the game's, or under
  your frame.

## Action bars

Five bars of the game's own secure buttons, so your spells and keys stay
where they are. The main bar follows your stance, form or stealth. Buttons
per row, size, spacing, fade out of combat. Plus a thin experience bar, the
game's menu and bag buttons in two neat rows, and your money, always shown.

## Nameplates

A slim bar over each monster and player in the game's own health colors,
with the name; your target framed in your color and a little bigger. Cast
bars, your debuffs, threat colors and enemy players in class color, each
your choice.

## Your look

- Your color: Pathwise blue, your class color, or any color you pick. Six
  color schemes, every color your own, and codes to share a scheme.
- Tooltips with a flat ground and a border in the item's quality or the
  player's class color; Inter for all the game's text; the game's windows
  on a flat ground with your accent along the top.
- Smooth motion, crisp one-pixel lines at any UI scale, and one button to
  move everything at once.
- Settings for every character, or each its own. Options: `/pwui` or the
  minimap button.
