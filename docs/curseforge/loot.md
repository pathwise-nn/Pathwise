# Pathwise Loot

**What does this boss drop?**

A loot browser for World of Warcraft: Forever: every dungeon and raid boss,
what it drops, a search over all of it and a wishlist. **Works on its own**:
no other addon needed. With Pathwise Gear installed, it marks the upgrades for
your spec. With the other Pathwise addons, it uses the same accent color.

**Beta.** This is Pathwise Loot's first release and it has not yet been played
in Forever.

## The browser

- Every dungeon and raid in Forever, from the game's own boss list: dungeons
  by level, then raids.
- Each boss's drops: icon, name in its quality color, slot and levels. Hover
  for the item's tooltip. Shift-click links it in chat, Ctrl-click tries it on.
- Search every boss's loot by name.
- Show one slot, or only what your class can use.
- **Upgrade marks** from Pathwise Gear: how much better an item is for your
  spec.

## Your wishlist

Right-click any item to keep it on your wishlist (each character has its own).
The wishlist says which boss drops each item.

## It learns from your kills

What a boss drops comes from QuestieDB's open data for Forever. That data
knows dungeons well and raids less well. Every drop you see from a boss (its
corpse, your group's loot rolls) is added to that boss, so the lists fill in as
you play. There are no drop chances: Forever's data doesn't give any.

Item tooltips say which boss drops an item.

## Your look

Your accent color (Pathwise blue, your class color or any color), color
schemes, smooth motion and crisp lines, the same clean look as the other
Pathwise addons.

`/pwloot` opens the browser, and so does the minimap button (right-click for
options). `/pwloot search <words>` searches. Settings can be shared by every
character or kept per character.
