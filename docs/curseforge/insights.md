# Pathwise Insights

**How is your leveling really going?**

Your leveling numbers for World of Warcraft: Forever, with a tab for every
character on your account. An add-on for
[Pathwise](https://github.com/pathwise-nn/pathwise): install Pathwise too.

**Beta.** This is Pathwise Insights' first release, and it has not yet been
played in Forever.

## Overview

- **Your numbers:** time played, XP an hour, quests turned in, monsters
  killed, deaths, distance travelled, sessions and days played.
- **Records:**
  - fastest and longest level
  - best, longest and busiest session
  - biggest day and days in a row
  - fastest, longest and best-paying quest
- **When you play:** the hours of the day and the days of the week.
- **Your leveling:**
  - time and XP an hour for each route, zone and level
  - your slowest guide steps
  - profession skill-ups

## Map

- **A heat map** of every continent you levelled on, and of the zones you
  spent the most time in. Show the time you spent, or the XP an hour you
  earned.
- **Your path**, with flights shown separately.
- Drawn over the game's own maps. It zooms to where you went, or shows the
  whole map.

## Share with Pathwise

This is optional, and it helps make the guides better. Make a share code for
a character and paste it into our GitHub form or the Pathwise Discord. Shared
codes show us:

- steps that are slow, often skipped, or where players die
- quests that are slow for the XP they give
- quest givers and turn-ins that aren't where the guide points

The Share tab shows exactly what is in the code before you copy it. The code
never holds your character's name, realm or guild, your path on the map, or
anything about other players. Nothing is ever sent from the game.

## Your look

The same window as the Pathwise hub, with the Pathwise logo and your
faction's crest. It takes your theme (all 17 Pathwise themes, light and
dark) and your accent: Pathwise blue, your class color or any color. Drag
the corner grip to make it any size, and pin it in place.

Only the time you play counts: time AFK and loading screens are left out.
Recording can be turned off, and any character can be hidden or its numbers
deleted. Everything is saved on your own computer.

`/pwinsights` opens it, and so does the minimap button (right-click for the
map). `/pwinsights map` opens the map. It is also listed under Settings >
Pathwise suite.
