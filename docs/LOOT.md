# Pathwise Loot

What every dungeon and raid boss in World of Warcraft: Forever drops, by
**leight_nn & florp**. **Works on its own.** With Pathwise Gear installed it
marks the upgrades for your spec; with Pathwise, Pathwise UI, Pathwise Heal or
Pathwise Encounters it shares their accent color. `/pwloot` or its minimap
button opens it.

**Not yet played in game.** Everything below has been checked against
Blizzard's Forever UI source and the tests' client stub only.

## What it does

- **The loot browser**: instances on the left (your wishlist, then dungeons by
  level, then raids), the bosses in the middle, the chosen boss's loot on the
  right. A loot row shows the item's icon, its name in its quality color, its
  slot and levels, how often you have seen it drop, and an upgrade mark from
  Pathwise Gear. Hovering shows the game's item tooltip. Shift-click and
  Ctrl-click are the game's own (link in chat, try it on).
- **Search** every boss's loot by item name (`/pwloot search <words>`, or the
  box at the top).
- **Filters**: by slot, and "Only for my class" (the client's AllowableClass).
- **A wishlist per character**: right-click an item, or click its diamond.
- **"Drops from" on item tooltips** (`loot.tooltip`).
- **The drops you see** are kept per boss for every character (`loot.learn`,
  in `ns.Data("seen")`). They fill in bosses the data knows little about, and
  bosses it does not know at all.
- With Pathwise Gear: `Pathwise.Gear.IsUpgrade(link)` for items the client has
  loaded (`loot.gear`).

Options: `/pwloot options` or a right-click on the minimap button.

## Where the loot comes from

`Pathwise_Loot/Data/Loot.lua` is generated by `npm run loot`
(`tools/data/loot.js` downloads the client tables, then `tools/data/loot.lua`
builds the file). Nothing in it is typed in by hand.

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| Instances, bosses, encounter ids | Forever's client tables `Map`, `Difficulty`, `DungeonEncounter` (wago.tools, product `wowf`) |
| Which monster a boss is | QuestieDB's Forever NPCs: the boss's name inside the instance; a longer or shorter form of it inside the instance ("Kirtonos" / "Kirtonos the Herald"); or the only NPC of that name, when it has no spawn |
| What a boss drops | QuestieDB's Forever items (`npcDrops`), corrected as `tools/data/build.lua` corrects them; items dropping from more than 12 monsters (world and trash drops) are left out |
| The items | Forever's `Item` table (slot, type; items the client lacks are left out) and `ItemSparse` (name, quality, levels, classes). `ItemSparse` holds only some items: for the rest the name and levels are QuestieDB's, and the quality comes from the game client in game |

**No drop chances**: QuestieDB has none, and Blizzard ships no loot tables for
Forever (the client has no Encounter Journal tables; Forever loads only the
journal's bootstrap).

**Gaps.** 55 of the 338 bosses have no monster in QuestieDB's data. Some are
councils ("The Seven", "The Four Horsemen"); most are in Forever's own and
Season of Discovery instances (Karazhan Crypts, Scarlet Enclave, Demon Fall
Canyon). Raids are thin: QuestieDB records few raid drops (Magmadar none). The
tool prints the full list each time it runs. These bosses fill in from the
drops players see.

**Not used**: AtlasLoot Classic. Its licence is GPL-2.0 with no "or any later
version" grant, which can't be combined with Pathwise's GPL-3.0-only, and its
data is Classic, TBC and Wrath, not Forever. None of its code, data, art or
name is in Pathwise Loot. cmangos' loot tables (classic-db) are not used either:
they describe patch 1.12, and its `COPYRIGHT.md` restricts altering the
Blizzard-derived content.

## How drops are learnt (Forever 1.60)

| Read | From | Note |
|---|---|---|
| The group's loot rolls | `LOOT_HISTORY_UPDATE_DROP`, `C_LootHistory.GetSortedInfoForDrop` | Forever loads the game's per-boss loot history (`[Game]\LootHistory.lua [AllowLoadGameType camelot]`) |
| Loot a player gets | `ENCOUNTER_LOOT_RECEIVED` | Carries the encounter id |
| The loot window | `LOOT_OPENED`, `GetNumLootItems`, `GetLootSlotType`, `GetLootSlotLink`, `GetLootSourceInfo` | `GetLootSourceInfo` is not used by Forever's own UI: called only if the client has it (**unverified**). A corpse counts when its monster is a known boss; a chest counts for the boss killed in the last 2 minutes |
| Kills, a boss's monsters | `ENCOUNTER_START`, `ENCOUNTER_END` (success), `INSTANCE_ENCOUNTER_ENGAGE_UNIT`, `UnitGUID("boss1".."boss5")` | |

An item counts once per kill. Every value passes `ns.Readable`
(`issecretvalue`) first, and a secret value is not recorded.
